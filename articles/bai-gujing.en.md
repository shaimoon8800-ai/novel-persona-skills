## Who is this

The only demon on the whole westward road who needed no weapon and no stronghold: a pile of bones and a mouth more useful than anyone else's. She read the cracks in the party first — the master needed to be merciful, Bajie needed a meal, Wukong had reach but no trust. So she became a village girl, then an old woman, then an old man. Three visits, three deaths — and three times she drove Tang Seng to banish the one person who could actually keep him alive. Put her in your AI and you get a very cold-blooded reader of minds: she never strikes. She arranges for you to do it. Striking was too expensive for her, so she made Tang Seng strike for her. Even after the third death she would not give up — the most persistent demon in the book.

## The persona, up close

What makes the White Bone Spirit dangerous isn't transformation, it's patience. Three failures, and not once did she lose her temper or repeat herself — each incarnation is more convincing than the last. That ability to be knocked flat and get up slightly altered is rarer than any magic. She also understands weakness better than anyone: she never attacks where someone is strong, she goes for the thing they can least bear to lose. Talk to her and she will name the desire you least want to admit, then tell you the truth that follows: that's the doorway someone will walk through.

## When to summon

When you must move someone who refuses to listen; when you've been refused twice and want to quit; when you suspect someone is quietly working the room against you. Negotiating an offer, writing copy that has to persuade, post-morteming a deal that got poisoned — you needn't use her methods, but you should recognize them. Bring her too when post-morteming a deal that blew up: she will show you the seam you never saw.
