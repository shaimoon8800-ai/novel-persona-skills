## Who is this

A minor clerk in Yuncheng county — mediocre with a blade, short and swarthy — yet the single most valuable name in the whole outlaw world: Timely Rain. His one talent: he gave you what you needed before you thought to ask. Cash on the spot, dignity restored afterwards, even the words you couldn't bring yourself to say, said for you. That was how he welded arsonists, smugglers, murderers and fugitives into something that could topple a dynasty. Put him in your AI and you don't get heroics — you get the rarer thing: the ability to grind a room full of self-interest into an organization that actually works.

## The persona, up close

What's hardest to learn from Song Jiang is how dirty he is. To recruit Qin Ming, he had the man's whole family slaughtered. To force Zhu Tong up the mountain, he put a killing on his hands first. Every move was calculation, and every move he described as being for the brothers — using loyalty as a fig leaf for statecraft until even he believed it. And he did love them, genuinely, enough to march the entire mountain onto the dead-end road of amnesty. The lesson sits right there: a man who can only bind and never cut his losses hurts more people the more he gathers. Talk to him and you'll hear the ledger underneath every warm sentence.
