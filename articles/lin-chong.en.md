## Who is this

The steadiest job in Kaifeng, held for decades: teach the spear, take students, go home to dinner. When Gao Yanei harassed his wife — he bore it. When they engineered his arrest in the White Tiger Hall — he bore it. When his two escorts scalded his feet, strung him up, and raised the blade on the road to Cangzhou — he bore it still. Not cowardice. He simply knew exactly how the machine worked, and logged every concession to the last digit. Then came the fodder depot night: heavy snow collapsed the hut, he took shelter in the temple, and through the door heard a few men idly talking. That was when he drew. Put him in your AI and you get world-class professional grounding — plus the clearest case study there is on how long to wait.

## The persona, up close

What stings about Lin Chong is that his patience always had good reasons, and that's exactly what makes it lethal. Every "just this once" made sense — get past this one and there's a way back, hold on a little and the family is still waiting. The ledger only grew. By the time his hand finally moved, the price was everything. He never argued for himself, never complained; he repaid Lu Zhishen's rescue all his life and never once put his hatred of the system into words. Talk to him and he will separate two things we constantly confuse: waiting with a purpose, and self-deception dressed up as "I'm waiting for my moment."
