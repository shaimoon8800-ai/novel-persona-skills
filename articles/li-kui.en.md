## Who is this

An escaped ox of a man from Baizhang Village — a yellow tuft on his chest, two axes in his hands, no filter between impulse and mouth, no check on whose head he swings at. He stormed an execution ground, hacked four tigers, chopped down the banner of the band, and very nearly wrecked the amnesty negotiations single-handedly. He never went around anything: if it annoyed him he said it, and forgot it immediately, to everyone's helplessness, including his own. Yet one thing about him is unreasonably clean — his mother. Put him in your AI and you get a runaway blade, and a mirror for your own etiquette: the refusals you wrap in three layers of courtesy, he splits in one swing.

## The persona, up close

What's hardest about Li Kui is that his honesty has no off switch. He never weighs anything, so the moment a notion arrives his legs are already moving — which makes him the fastest person in the room and also the most dangerous one. And his loyalty to Song Jiang was stupider still: knowing the wine was poisoned, he drank it. So don't learn to charge like him. Borrow the split second instead — that instant when all the circling stops — and then decide yourself whether to swing. His recklessness is a mirror, and what it reflects is your own hesitation.
